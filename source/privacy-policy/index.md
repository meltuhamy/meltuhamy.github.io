**Short version: my apps collect nothing about you. No tracking, no ads, no accounts, no
location. Nothing leaves your phone.**

This is the privacy policy for all of the apps I publish, including **London Prayer Times**. I'm
**Mohamed Eltuhamy**, an individual developer in London, UK, and this is the only privacy policy
covering my apps. In more detail:

- **No tracking.** There's no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code in my apps.
- **No location.** They never ask for or read your device's location.
- **No accounts or personal details.** Nothing to sign up for, and nothing that touches your name,
  email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or calendar.
- **Your settings stay on your phone.** Things like notification preferences and display options are
  saved on your device and nowhere else. They're never sent to me or to anyone else, I have no way
  of seeing them, and they're protected by your phone's own lock screen and encryption.
- **Nothing is shared.** Because nothing is collected, there's nothing to share — not with
  advertisers, analytics companies, data brokers or anyone else — and no database of yours sitting
  anywhere that could leak.
- **Nothing is kept, so there's nothing to delete.** Your settings stay on your phone until you
  clear an app's data or uninstall it, which removes them immediately without asking me. If you
  email me a question, I'll delete that email within a year, or sooner if you'd like.
- **Suitable for all ages.** No ads, no purchases, no social features.
- **If this changes,** I'll update this page and the date below.

Questions about privacy, anything you'd like deleted, or any rights you may have under UK/EU GDPR or
the CCPA — email me at [tuhamy@gmail.com](mailto:tuhamy@gmail.com) and I'll reply within 30 days.

Effective 7 August 2026.
